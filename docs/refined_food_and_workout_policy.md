# RootedFit Food and Workout Content Policy

## Input taxonomy

RootedFit separates **ready-made meals**, **raw ingredients**, and **fruits**. Meal suggestions belong in “Meals you enjoy”; examples include jollof rice, moi moi, amala and ewedu, eba and soup, or pepper soup. Ingredient suggestions belong in “Ingredients within your reach”; examples include beans, yam, parboiled rice, tomato, onion, leafy greens, fish, spices, and cooking oils. Fruit belongs only in the fruit section and is capped at four favourites to keep preferences decisive and usable.

The main plan is a concise daily summary. It must show every meal slot created by the user’s one-, two-, or three-meal selection, plus snack ideas where relevant. The Meals tab is the full recipe library: ingredients, method, drink option, equipment note, and storage note appear there rather than overloading the summary.

## Sweet-tooth choice

Sweet-tooth preferences are never treated as a failure or ban. The product supports no special handling, satisfying alternatives such as fruit with yoghurt or a homemade cocoa-oat snack, and selected portion guidance after a balanced meal. This is a practical habit option, not a clinical nutrition protocol.

## External home-workout resources

The workout library maps the user’s primary focus to optional home follow-along sessions: low-impact home cardio for a weight-loss focus, at-home strength for a weight-gain or toning focus, Pilates and yoga for core/mobility, and manageable bodyweight or yoga sessions for energy and consistency. The resource selection uses publicly available YouTube sessions surfaced through search; direct viewing may depend on user network and YouTube availability. [1] [2] [3]

The app labels these as optional sessions and avoids promises of spot reduction, guaranteed weight change, or medical outcomes. Users should pause or select a gentler option when a session is not appropriate for them.

| Primary focus | RootedFit session pattern |
|---|---|
| Weight loss | Low-impact home cardio plus beginner bodyweight movement |
| Weight gain | At-home strength plus beginner bodyweight strength |
| Strength and toning | Strength session plus full-body Pilates option |
| Core and mobility | Pilates plus beginner yoga/mobility option |
| Energy and consistency | Manageable beginner bodyweight or gentle yoga option |
| Body-composition habits | Low-impact cardio and strength option without body-change promises |
