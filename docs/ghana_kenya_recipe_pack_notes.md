# Ghana and Kenya Recipe Pack Notes

## Ghana pack

The Ghana starter pack uses Waakye as a rice-and-bean anchor. A recipe source describes the basic structure as black-eyed peas or cow beans, rice, and sorghum leaves, with the leaves simmered first to colour the cooking water before beans and rice are cooked in sequence. [1] RootedFit will simplify this into clearly measured household portions and will suggest a plain tomato-onion accompaniment only where it fits a user’s stated ingredients and dietary exclusions.

## Kenya pack

The Kenya starter pack uses Sukuma Wiki with Ugali and Githeri as familiar, flexible anchors. A Kenyan Sukuma Wiki recipe describes sturdy greens such as collards, kale, or spinach cooked with onion, garlic, tomato, and a little broth; it is commonly served alongside maize-flour Ugali. [2] RootedFit will retain those core ingredients and show substitutions in plain language rather than assume every household has the same greens or cooking fat.

## Product boundary

These recipe packs are regional starting points rather than an assertion that every household in Ghana or Kenya eats the same way. Users continue to control favourite meals, local ingredients, restrictions, dislikes, food storage constraints, and meal frequency. Recipes must remain editable and never claim health outcomes.

## Local interaction behaviour

The Meals tab stores a swap by day and meal-slot key, then cycles the replacement through the active country pack without changing a user’s meal-frequency setting. The Workout Library stores whether an individual follow-along session is saved and the calendar date on which the user marked it complete. Both interaction records stay on the device in the MVP and are removed with the local demo-profile reset.
