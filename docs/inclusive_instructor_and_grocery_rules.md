# Inclusive Instructor and Grocery Rules

RootedFit treats a user’s gender preference as a **default recommendation signal**, not a restriction. Users selecting a male identity see a male-led follow-along option first; users selecting a female identity see a female-led option first; and all users can open the alternative instructor link. This avoids assuming that a person can only prefer one type of instructor.

The male-led alternatives use the publicly available Body Coach TV by Joe Wicks follow-along catalogue, including its multi-day and introductory home sessions. The channel surfaced several day-specific home workouts and beginner sessions during review. RootedFit retains existing female-led and mixed alternatives so every user has a visible choice.

The grocery checklist is deliberately separate from recipe portions. Recipe cards may say “½ cup rice” or “¼ onion” because those describe preparation; a household shopping list must instead say “rice — 1 kg or a smaller bag,” “onions — 5–6 medium,” or “cooking oil — 1 small bottle.” Where household size, storage, or market cadence differs, the app frames units as practical purchase choices rather than exact clinical or prescriptive targets.
