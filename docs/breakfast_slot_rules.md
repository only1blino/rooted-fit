# RootedFit Breakfast Slot Rules

RootedFit treats the breakfast slot in two- and three-meal schedules as a **lighter, quick-preparation, protein-forward meal**. It must not schedule heavy swallows or large starch-led dishes such as amala, semo, or pounded yam for breakfast. Those dishes remain available for later meal slots when they fit the user’s food preferences and local cooking resources.

The planning engine gives breakfast priority to familiar, adaptable formats such as egg-and-vegetable meals, beans with a small bread or plantain portion, yoghurt or milk with oats and fruit, tofu with vegetables, and pap or oats paired with a clear protein source. These are patterns rather than medical prescriptions: every recipe remains editable, respects local availability and dietary restrictions, and is framed as general wellness support.

The British Dietetic Association lists beans, lentils, chickpeas, eggs, fish, tofu, nuts, and seeds among protein food options, including options that can be added to cereal or porridge. The NHS breakfast collection also includes practical formats such as beans on toast, overnight oats, egg dishes, yoghurt with oats and fruit, and vegetable omelettes. [1] [2]
