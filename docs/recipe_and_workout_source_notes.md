# RootedFit Recipe and Workout Source Notes

## Recipe direction

The research confirms that practical Nigerian and West African home cooking has a strong source base for coherent recipe cards, including jollof rice, beans porridge, and familiar soups. Serious Eats’ Nigerian jollof recipe describes a tomato-and-red-pepper rice dish seasoned with ingredients such as curry powder, thyme, bay leaf, ginger, and garlic. [1] Yummy Medley and Dash of Jazz provide reference material for Nigerian beans porridge/stewed beans. [2] [3]

RootedFit’s content update should therefore use recognisable complete dishes with logical pairings—such as jollof rice with chicken and a vegetable side, beans porridge with plantain, or a familiar soup with a stated swallow—not improvised pairings such as rice with whole-grain bread. Recipe cards should provide measured ingredient lists, a small number of ordered preparation steps, substitutions that preserve the dish, and explicit equipment assumptions.

## External workout resources

Two direct YouTube videos were verified in-browser for use as optional external workout resources. Move With Nicole’s **30 MIN PILATES CORE WORKOUT || At-Home Pilates Abs (Moderate)** is a 33-minute Pilates core session. [4] Yoga With Adriene’s **30-Minute Yoga For Beginners | Start Yoga Here** is a beginner-oriented 30-minute practice focused on breath and foundational elements. [5] These links should be presented as optional external resources, with clear labels for duration, format, and equipment expectations.

## Content policy

The supplied fitness and meal-preparation references are valuable for their specific, direct, encouraging tone. RootedFit will adopt that clarity without claiming spot reduction, guaranteed body changes, “flat stomach” outcomes, or other unsupported medical/body-composition effects. Weight change focuses will be implemented as non-clinical routine directions, with progressive and sustainable meal and movement patterns rather than prescriptive medical advice.

## Implemented content mapping

The current Nigeria-first rotation now presents complete, recognisable home dishes: Nigerian jollof rice with chicken and cabbage slaw; beans porridge with ripe plantain; yam and egg sauce; efo riro with fish and a small semo portion; moi moi with pap and cucumber; catfish pepper soup with boiled sweet potato; and Ofada-style rice with tomato stew and vegetables. Each card includes practical ingredient quantities and ordered preparation steps. Dietary restrictions and dislikes are used to remove incompatible recipes before the weekly rotation is assembled.

The focus selector now includes **Weight loss** and **Weight gain** alongside consistency, energy, strength/toning, core/mobility, and body-composition habits. These focuses shape encouragement and variety only; they do not generate crash diets, guaranteed outcomes, or condition-specific advice.
