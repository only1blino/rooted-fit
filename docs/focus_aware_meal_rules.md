# Focus-Aware Meal and Grocery Rules

RootedFit uses a general wellness approach, not diagnosis or prescribed dietary treatment. All plans begin with the same safety baseline: adequate, balanced, moderated, and diverse meals shaped by local foods, culture, and stated availability. [1]

| Focus | In-app meal adjustment | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Weight-loss focus | Preserve familiar meals while emphasising vegetables, beans, leaner protein options where available, and a measured staple portion. | Do not promise a weight outcome, eliminate food groups, or set extreme portions. |
| Weight-gain focus | Keep the same local meal patterns while adding a planned snack and a modest extra portion of a staple, beans, dairy/alternatives, nuts, or seeds where suitable. | Use gradual, balanced additions rather than sugary foods or beverage-only calories. [2] |
| Strength and toning | Retain a regular local staple with an intentional protein component and a vegetable/green side. | Do not imply targeted body transformation. |
| Energy, consistency, and mobility | Prioritise a practical meal rhythm, food storage fit, and components that the user actually has. | Do not make treatment claims. |

The grocery list must be built by de-duplicating ingredient lines from the planned weekly recipe cards and attaching quantities where known. General placeholder categories such as “a grain” are not shown. The app keeps restrictions and disliked foods as exclusions in every focus.
