# Final Polish Reference Notes

## HomeFitnessforLeanPhysique.pdf — early layout and content findings

The document uses a highly conversational coaching style with three reusable presentation patterns that fit RootedFit well. First, it breaks guidance into short problem-response sections, which makes the content feel personal rather than generic. Second, it uses bold subheads such as technique names, balance notes, and quick blueprints, which helps the reader scan specific actions quickly. Third, it turns advice into concrete numbered steps, portions, and timing cues instead of vague wellness language.

The app should borrow the **specificity** and **scan-friendly structure** but not copy unsupported physiological or body-shaping claims. Good patterns to adopt include: a short daily focus headline, one clear practical instruction block, one “why this helps today” note, and a concise fallback or substitution section when the user lacks an ingredient or tool.

## StopStartingOver — motivation and affirmation findings

The motivation reference uses a compelling **“do not start over”** narrative. Its strongest reusable element is the flat-tire metaphor: one missed habit is a small interruption, not a reason to abandon every habit. It also demonstrates the value of empathetic, direct language that names everyday constraints such as fatigue, family responsibilities, deadlines, and imperfect meals.

RootedFit should turn this into a clearly labelled tracker feature rather than hide it in a general summary. Each day should show: a visible **Daily affirmation**, a one-sentence **smallest next action**, and a compassionate **reset reminder** after an incomplete day. The app will use this behavioural framing while avoiding the document’s specific medical, hormonal, or body-composition claims.

## Implemented tracker polish

The tracker now exposes its encouragement at the top of the screen: a rotating daily affirmation, a concrete one-small-action prompt, and a separately labelled continuity reminder. This makes the motivation visible before users enter steps or measurements and reinforces the principle that a missed action does not require a wholesale restart.

The requested measurement pairings are now explicit. Users can select **feet/inches for body measurements with kilograms for weight**, or **centimetres for body measurements with pounds for weight**. RootedFit stores canonical centimetres and kilograms internally so changing the display pairing does not lose a user’s historical records.
