# RootedFit location-aware meal pack notes

## Verified starting points

The Kenyan pack should recognise sukuma wiki as a familiar greens preparation using sukuma wiki or collard greens, tomato, onion, oil, and salt. It is commonly paired with ugali and can be adapted with spinach when that is the available green. The South African pack should recognise samp and beans (also called umngqusho or isistambu) as a comfort-food base prepared with samp, beans, onion, garlic, tomato, and curry seasoning.

The app will treat recipe packs as practical, flexible starting points rather than claims about every household in a city. City text will select a local sub-pack where RootedFit has one, and the onboarding ingredient list remains the user’s final local-availability signal.
