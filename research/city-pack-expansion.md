# City-pack expansion notes

The current beta-feedback table contains no voluntarily provided city names, so the next packs extend practical coverage rather than claiming to reflect named tester locations. New aliases should make future beta feedback easier to map without collecting personal profile data on the server.

| City | Practical food cues for RootedFit | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Kigali, Rwanda | Beans, cassava, sweet potatoes, plantains, corn, peas, millet, greens, tilapia, sambaza, avocado, banana, mango, pineapple, and papaya. | Together Women Rise, “Customs and Cuisine of Rwanda” |
| Lusaka, Zambia | Nshima made with maize meal, paired with a vegetable, bean, fish, or poultry relish (ndiwo); greens, beans, groundnuts, and fish are practical supporting cues. | Mwizenge Tembo, “Nshima and Ndiwo” |
| Dakar, Senegal | Rice, fish, millet, sweet potatoes, cassava, black-eyed peas, vegetables, peanuts, and fresh fruit; thieboudienne, mafe, and domoda are useful meal cues. | The Flavor Vortex, “International Cooking: Food from Senegal” |

These cues are suggestions—not availability guarantees. The app must continue to use the person’s own ingredient and equipment inputs as the final filter.
